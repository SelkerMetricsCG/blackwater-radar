Terms of use

  • Blackwater Radar is a free weather map made from public data. It is provided as is, without any warranty.
  • Some webcam images are thumbnails of YouTube live streams, shown through the YouTube API Services. By using this map you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Those thumbnails and their live status come from YouTube; each camera's name, map position and owner link come from this map.
  • Blackwater Radar is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google, or by the owners of the cameras it shows.

Privacy

  • This site has no accounts or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics or tracking code, and sets no cookies. The site itself collects, stores and shares no information about you.
  • YouTube API Services. This site uses YouTube API Services to learn which YouTube webcams are live and to get their thumbnails. It asks once an hour, in the job that builds the map's data, and sends nothing about you to YouTube. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Stored on your device. The page keeps your region, the panel sections you folded and any map you saved as your default in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device; clearing this site's data removes them.
  • Your location. On a first visit the page may ask for your location, only to open the region you are in. It is used inside your browser and sent nowhere.
  • Content from other servers. Your browser loads map tiles from Esri and OpenTopoMap, weather data from this site's file store, and each webcam image straight from its owner's server, including YouTube's servers for YouTube webcams. When you click the map, the clicked point is sent to the National Weather Service's API for a forecast. Like any web server, these see your IP address and browser details, under their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting. The page is served by Cloudflare, which handles each request the way any web host does.
