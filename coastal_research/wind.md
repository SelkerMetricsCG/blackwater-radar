# Wind: observations and forecasts for the Coastal section (research, 2026-10-13)

Scope: wind at the water's edge and over the inland salt water, window 41.0-49.7 N, 126.6-112.5 W, plus the
Columbia Gorge (inland, labelled as such). Free feeds only. Everything marked **fetched** was requested today,
2026-10-13 (19:00-20:00 UTC), with `User-Agent: coastal-research/0.1`, 20 s timeout, a handful of requests per host.
Everything marked *read* comes from documentation or search results only and needs a live check before it is built on.

## Summary

- The site already has the three pieces a kiter needs most, and they only need pointing at the water: NDFD
  `ds.wspd/wdir/wgust.bin` (pacnwest, hourly) is already decoded in `forecast.py`; api.weather.gov observations are
  already pulled; HRRR via NOMADS subregion is already used for freezing level. Add coastal station ids and a per-spot
  timeline and the wind section is mostly plumbing.
- Two new free observation feeds are worth adding: **NOAA CO-OPS wind at tide stations** (17 stations with
  anemometers in the window, 6-minute data, JSON, CORS `*`, literally on the water: Westport, Port Townsend, Port
  Angeles, Astoria, South Beach, Friday Harbor, Cherry Point, Tacoma, Bremerton, Toke Point, La Push...) and
  **Environment Canada SWOB-ML** hourly XML (Race Rocks, Point Atkinson, Esquimalt, Tsawwassen ferry, White Rock,
  Victoria Gonzales... 15 stations in the Strait/south BC box).
- api.weather.gov quietly serves **CWOP citizen stations** (ids `Cnnnn`, `Dnnnn`, `Ennnn`) and **ODOT RWIS** (`ODTnn`)
  with wind and gust: Port Townsend, Bainbridge, Hansville, Sequim (Dungeness), Anacortes, Ferndale (Birch Bay),
  Ocean Shores, I-84 at Cascade Locks. No registration, open data.
- Best free high-resolution model for the whole window, including the Strait and Vancouver Island, is Environment
  Canada's **HRDPS 2.5 km**, and MSC GeoMet serves it as a **WCS GeoTIFF subset** (332 KB for the whole window, one
  variable, one hour) and as point values via WMS GetFeatureInfo. No GRIB decoding, no account.
- **HRRR** wind/gust/MSLP for the window is 813 KB per forecast hour through the NOMADS subregion filter (4 GRIB
  messages), versus ~5.4 MB per hour by byte range from AWS. **NBM** (hourly runs, 10 m wind, direction and gust with
  spread) is on AWS with `.idx` files; its NBH text bulletin gives 25-hour hourly wind/gust/direction for every NDBC
  buoy and C-MAN station (46041 confirmed) in one file.
- NWS marine zone forecasts and Small Craft/Gale advisories are already reachable through api.weather.gov
  (`/zones/forecast/PZZ135/forecast`, `/alerts/active?area=PZ`); 26 PZZ zones cover the window.
- Proprietary spot networks (iKitesurf/Tempest, Windy, Windguru, Windfinder, Synoptic) are not usable on free terms;
  the one real gap this leaves is the east Gorge (Rufus, Arlington, Doug's Beach), where the only sensors are
  WeatherFlow's.

## What to use first (ranked)

1. **api.weather.gov observations, extended to the coastal id list** (ASOS + CWOP + ODT RWIS above). Zero new
   infrastructure, open data, CORS, includes gust and pressure. Gives the Gorge west/mid sensors (KTTD, K4S2, KDLS,
   ODT78, TBELL) and the Sound (KPAE, C1035, E0123, C5456, KTIW).
2. **NOAA CO-OPS wind product** for the 16 tide stations with anemometers: the only free sensors that are literally at
   the water (Westport, Port Townsend, Port Angeles, Friday Harbor, Cherry Point, Tacoma, Astoria, South Beach,
   Charleston, Port Orford, Crescent City). 6-minute, tiny JSON, CORS.
3. **NDFD wspd/wdir/wgust, sampled per spot** (already downloaded hourly): the human-edited forecast; add the
   VP.004-007 wspd/wdir for days 4-7.
4. **HRRR via the NOMADS subregion filter** (813 KB/hour for u, v, gust, MSLP): hourly timeline and the pressure
   gradient curve for the Gorge and Sound, in the same code shape as `freezing.py`.
5. **HRDPS via GeoMet WCS** (332 KB GeoTIFF per variable-hour for the whole window): the Strait, San Juans, Georgia
   Strait and VI spots, and the second model everywhere. Attribution line required.
6. **NWS marine zone forecasts + PZ alerts** through api.weather.gov: text period and advisory badge per spot.
7. **Environment Canada SWOB-ML hourly XML** for Race Rocks, Point Atkinson, Esquimalt, Tsawwassen, White Rock, Victoria
   Gonzales (Jericho/Squamish-side users). Attribution line required.
8. **NDBC realtime2** for DESW1, WPOW1, SISW1, 46041, 46029, 46050, 46087, 46088 (shared with the waves agent).
9. **NBM NBH bulletin** (28 MB hourly, buoys/airports only) and **NBM core GRIB by byte range** for a blended third
   opinion with spread; only if the hourly job has room.
10. **ECMWF open data / GFS** for a days-4-10 outlook row (CC-BY-4.0 attribution for ECMWF; curl, not urllib).

Not usable on free terms: WeatherFlow/iKitesurf (TempestONE subscription for public stations), Windy (test tier is
shuffled data; public weather apps not permitted), Windguru (no API), Windfinder (paid API; widgets are iframes),
Synoptic (paid), MADIS mesonet (registration + redistribution notification). WSDOT RWIS is free but needs Chris to
request an Access Code by email.
